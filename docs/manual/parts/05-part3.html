Chapter 9 Analytics & Operations Report
A formal seven-section report for the Municipal Mayor and the Market Board.

The Analytics module compiles a complete executive operations report from live records. It is not a dashboard with charts bolted on — it is a document, laid out for printing, with a masthead, a reference number and seven numbered sections in the order a board expects to read them.

description Report masthead
Seal The Tanauan municipal seal, top left
Reference PMRMS-AR-YYYYMMDD, generated from the date
Title Public Market Operations Report
Timestamp Date and time of generation, Philippine Standard Time

9.1 Anatomy of the report

Figure 9.1 — The seven sections. Sections I–IV answer “is the market full and are the leases sound”; V answers “is the money coming in”; VI–VII answer “is the place compliant and is the office keeping its records”.

9.2 Reading each section

summarize I · Executive Summary

Five headline cards, each with a progress meter: Stall Occupancy Rate, Active Tenancies, Applications Pending, Collection Efficiency Rate, and Open Violations & Demerit Points. If the board reads one page, this is it.

bar_chart II · Stall Utilization

The same stacked chart as Figure 3.2, backed by a table giving exact counts per status with proportion bars. Use this to justify a request for stall rehabilitation.

how_to_reg III · Applicant Processing

Shows where applications are getting stuck. A large Incomplete share is a documentation problem; a large Pending Review share is an officer-time problem.

apartment IV · Tenancy & Rental Coverage

Section-by-section occupancy and monthly rent roll. This is the section that shows which part of the market is carrying the revenue.

payments V · Utility Billing & Collection

Electricity and water consumption, amounts billed, amounts collected and the outstanding balance. Collection efficiency in section I is derived from these figures.

gavel VI–VII · Compliance & Activity

Recent citations with their status, then a breakdown of logbook entries by type — evidence that inspections and collections are actually being recorded, not just performed.

9.3 Exporting & presenting

Export Report downloads a complete JSON file (civic-market-core-report.json) holding every figure behind the report. Use it to archive the month, to hand data to another office, or to build a chart the report does not include.

To present the report, print it directly (§2.6). Because it is laid out as a document, the printed output needs no reformatting.

bolt
The report is always live. There is no “generate” step and no stored version. Opening Analytics recompiles it from current records, so the reference number is the date you opened it — print or export on the day the board meets.
warning
Because it is live, a report printed before month-end collection differs from one printed after. Note the timestamp in the masthead when you file a copy.

Chapter 10 Logbook (Audit Trail)
The record of what was done, by which module, and when — written mostly without you.

The Logbook is both the daily activity ledger and the audit trail. Officers write inspections, incidents and announcements into it by hand; the system writes everything else into it automatically.

10.1 Log types

TypeColourUsed forTypical source
InspectionBlueStall inspections and permit checksMostly entered by hand by the Market Inspector

AnnouncementGrayPublic notices, schedule changes, advisoriesEntered by hand

10.2 What the system logs by itself

Every record-changing action writes a structured, timestamped entry. You never have to remember to log these:

  • Adding, editing or deleting a stall
  • Creating, editing or releasing a tenant
  • Ticking or unticking a month’s rent
  • Approving or rejecting an applicant
  • Assigning a stall to an approved applicant
  • Issuing, editing, paying or deleting a utility bill
  • Recording, amending or resolving a violation
  • Appointing, transferring or removing an officer
  • Issuing a stall / space verification slip
  • Importing, exporting or resetting the database
history
Rent entries name the figures. A collection entry records the tenant, the stall, the month, the amount collected and — where it applied — the 20% discount and the date it was settled on. That single line is usually enough to answer a query without opening the tenant record.

error
Deleting a log entry breaks the trail. The system warns you because the Logbook is the office’s audit record. Delete only a genuine mis-entry, and record why in a replacement entry.

Chapter 11 Market Assistant
Ask a question in plain language. The figures still come from the records.

The Market Assistant lets an officer ask about rent, stalls, bills, tenants and violations without building a filter. It runs entirely on the workstation — there is no online service behind it.

11.1 How the two-tier engine works

Figure 11.1 — The verification gate. The model never supplies a number. It may only re-word an answer the database already computed, and its wording is thrown away if any figure or name in it fails to match the computed facts.
verified
Data accuracy guarantee. Amounts, counts and names are always produced by the system’s own business logic — never generated by a language model. If the model’s phrasing disagrees with the computed facts on any figure, the phrasing is discarded and the verified record text is shown instead.

11.3 Reading an answer

labelSource badges

Every answer states where its wording came from:

  • From the records — computed directly from the database
  • Worded by model · figures verified — the model phrased it, and every figure passed the check
  • Model wording discarded — the phrasing failed the check; you are seeing the raw record text

All three are equally trustworthy on the numbers. The badge tells you about the prose, not the data.

touch_appWorking with the assistant

  • Suggestion chips — one tap for the questions asked most often
  • Composer bar — type any question in natural language
  • Navigation buttons — answers offer Open Tenants, Open Billing and similar, so you can act on what you just read
  • Model status — the header badge shows neurology a model is active, or database records-only mode
info
Records-only mode is not a degraded mode. Every one of the twelve intents above is answered identically without a model.
