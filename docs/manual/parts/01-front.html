About This Manual

This manual is the complete operating reference for the Public Market Rental Monitoring System (PMRMS) installed at the Market Office of the Municipality of Tanauan, Leyte. It documents every screen, every field and every business rule the system enforces, so that any officer assigned to the Market Office can run the market’s records without prior training.

groups Who this manual is for
  • Market Supervisor — approvals, officer board, verification slips
  • Market Collector — rent collection, utility billing, receipts
  • Market Inspector — stall inspections, violations, logbook
  • Records Officer — tenant and applicant records, backups
route How to find what you need
  • New to the system? Read Part I in full (Chapters 1–2).
  • Daily counter work? Chapters 5 and 7 cover rent and billing.
  • Need a rule or figure? Go straight to Appendix A.
  • Something is wrong? Appendix B lists fixes by symptom.
