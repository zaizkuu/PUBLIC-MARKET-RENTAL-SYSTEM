Chapter 6 Applicant Management
From the day a folder is filed to the day a stall key changes hands.

Applicant Management is the intake end of the market. It tracks each application from filing, through the six documentary requirements, to approval or rejection — and then hands the approved applicant straight to the stall assignment form, which creates the tenant record for you.

6.3 Approval & stall assignment

Open the review form
Click the applicant’s row, or the review action button. Here you can correct the applicant’s details and tick or untick each requirement as documents are presented.
Approve
With the requirements satisfied, click Approve. The Assign Stall form opens immediately — approval and assignment are one continuous action.
Assign a stall
The form lists only genuinely vacant stalls. If the applicant named a preferred stall and it is still free, it is pre-selected for you. Set:
  • Stall — from the vacancy list
  • Monthly Rent — the contracted amount for this stall
  • Section — filled in from the stall
The applicant’s name and mobile number carry over automatically.
Confirm
Click Assign & Create Tenant. In one step the system creates the tenant record, marks the stall Occupied, links the applicant record to the new tenant, and writes the logbook entry.
schedule
Approving without assigning. Click Skip for now to approve an applicant while no suitable stall is free. The approval stands and the stall can be assigned later from the same record.
warning
Check the rent before confirming. The Monthly Rent you enter here becomes the basis for every future rent collection and every 20% discount. Correcting it later does not restate months already collected.

Chapter 7 Utility Billing
Meter readings in, receipts out — for electricity and water across every stall.

Utility Billing is the meter-reading and billing engine. It computes consumption from two readings, applies the municipal rate, issues a numbered bill, and prints the receipt — one at a time, or four to a sheet for a whole section.

7.1 Rates & the billing formula

UtilityRateUnitLate surcharge
bolt Electricity₱11.50per kWh3.35%
water_drop Water₱25.00per cu.m10.00%
calculate The formula

Consumption = Current Reading − Previous Reading  (never below zero)
Amount Due = Consumption × Rate per Unit
Total if late = Amount Due × (1 + surcharge rate)

Figure 7.1 — How a bill is computed. Surcharge is never written into the bill record. It is recalculated whenever the receipt is printed, so a receipt always shows what is owed today while the ledger keeps what was originally billed.

7.2 Issuing a bill, step by step

Choose the utility
Toggle Electricity or Water. The rate, the unit label (kWh or cu.m) and the icon all change with it.
Choose the stall or the tenant
Pick a stall and the occupant, last recorded reading and meter number fill themselves in. Picking a tenant instead selects their stall.
Set the period covered
Enter Period From and Period To, or press Cover all of [Month] to snap to the first and last day of the current month.
Set the due date
Defaults to 15 days after the period end. Adjust it if the office is billing on a different cycle.
Enter the readings
Previous Reading is carried forward from the last bill on record. Type the Current Reading. The system rejects negative readings and warns if the current reading is below the previous one.
Check the live summary
A summary card shows stall, tenant, meter number, period, both readings, consumption, rate and amount due — and warns you with the computed surcharge if the due date has already passed.
Save or print
  • Save to Records — posts the bill, writes the logbook entry, and records the meter number against the stall if it was not already stored
  • Print Receipt — opens the print preview straight away
  • Clear — empties the calculator without saving
content_copy
Duplicate detection. If a bill already exists for the same stall, utility and billing period, the system stops and asks whether you meant to issue a second one. This catches the most common billing error — the same section read twice in a month.

7.6 Late surcharges

warning Surcharge rules
  • Electricity: 3.35% of the billed amount, once the due date has passed.
  • Water: 10.00% of the billed amount, once the due date has passed.
  • Surcharge is computed dynamically, at print time. It appears on the receipt only if the bill is past due on the day you print.
  • The stored ledger amount is never changed. Historic records stay exactly as billed, while the receipt in the tenant’s hand shows what is owed today.
  • A bill turns Overdue by itself the day after its due date — no month-end process is run.
info
Why the two figures differ. Officers sometimes ask why the register shows ₱450.00 while the receipt says ₱495.00. The register is the bill; the receipt is the demand as of today. Both are correct, and Appendix A records the rule.

Chapter 8 Violations Register
Citations, demerit points and the record of who keeps offending.

The Violations Register records offences committed by tenants and by non-tenants trading in the market, assigns demerit points as a measure of severity, and tracks each case through to resolution.

8.1 Overview & demerit points

TotalCitations recorded
OpenStill unresolved
ResolvedCases closed
PointsOpen demerits

A dedicated Repeat Offenders panel ranks tenants by their total unresolved demerit points. Points from resolved citations drop out of the ranking, so the list is always a picture of live compliance risk rather than a permanent black mark.

8.3 Recording & resolving a citation

Click + Record Violation
Opens the citation form.
Fill in the citation
  • Party Cited — pick from the tenant list, or type any name for a non-tenant
  • Demerit Points — the severity, as a number
  • Violation Issue — one of the eight standard issues, or your own wording
  • Status — Open, or Resolved if it was settled on the spot
  • Date Recorded / Date Resolved
  • Officer Notes — free text; this is where the account of what happened belongs
Save
The citation is recorded and a logbook entry written.
task_alt Resolving

Open Edit Violation and set the status to Resolved. The resolution date is stamped for you. Reopening a case clears that date again. Demerit points and notes can be amended at any time.

sort Sorting & export

The register lists open cases first, then newest recorded date. Export CSV downloads every citation; Print List prints the filtered table for the inspector’s rounds.

lightbulb
Write the notes as if for a hearing. Officer Notes are the only free-text account of the offence. Record what was seen, when, and who was present — the badge and the points will not carry a case on their own.
