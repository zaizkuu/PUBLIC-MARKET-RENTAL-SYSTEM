# Tanauan, Leyte — proposed stall-map basis

Research date: 19 October 2026 (Asia/Manila).

This proposal is a rental-system planning aid for **Tanauan Public Market, San Roque, Tanauan, Leyte**. It is not a measured floor plan or a verified record of the current interior. Online evidence supports the location, historic external character, and approximate arrangement of mapped market footprints. It does not establish current stall boundaries, official stall numbers, floor assignments, entrances, or internal circulation.

## Proposed rental-system representation

- Present the proposal separately from the saved operational map.
- Retain the two mapped blocks as location context. Any interior subdivision, category assignment, aisle, entrance marker, or numbered bay is a design proposal pending verification.
- Use the system's existing stall records and identifiers when displaying rentals. Do not create official-looking inventory, tenant assignments, rent values, or occupancy from photo interpretation.
- Identify proposed stall placement clearly. A convenient arrangement of saved stall records does not establish their real positions.
- Keep navigation/route claims disabled for the proposal until a walk-through establishes connected aisles, entrances, stairs, and access restrictions.
- Label the diagram **Proposed layout · not to scale · positions require site verification**. Label any illustrative bay count as a proposal rather than an official market total.

## Information needed to confirm the map

Match an up-to-date market-office stall register to a dated walk-through or approved municipal floor plan. Verify the perimeter, building/floor names, every stall number and boundary, shared areas, internal passages, and public entrances. Record any temporary relocation caused by renovation. Confirm dimensions and floor assignments before publishing area or capacity figures. Obtain the current renovation/as-built drawings from the Municipal Engineering Office or market administrator if available.

Those checks turn the proposed layout into a dependable rental map; online exterior imagery alone cannot supply them.
